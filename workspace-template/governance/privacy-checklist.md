# Privacy and Compliance Checklist

Complete before uploading data or enabling an external AI provider.

- [ ] Data owner and authorized users are documented.
- [ ] Ethics approval, consent, or waiver status is documented where applicable.
- [ ] Direct identifiers have been removed.
- [ ] Indirect re-identification risks have been assessed.
- [ ] Data use agreements and publication restrictions have been checked.
- [ ] External AI destination, region, retention, and training policy are approved.
- [ ] Only the minimum necessary fields will be transferred.
- [ ] The transfer decision and approver are recorded.
- [ ] Local backups and access controls are configured.
